# Community Allotment Work Plan

Saturday volunteers will meet at the allotment gate at 09:00. The first task is repairing the east fence, followed by compost turning and watering. Plot 14 will host dwarf beans, while the north beds are reserved for herbs and pollinator flowers.

Tools must be signed out from the red cabinet. Rain moves the mower demonstration indoors to the community hall. The harvest is weighed before produce is donated, and the shed key is returned to the coordinator before leaving.
