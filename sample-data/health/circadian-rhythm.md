# Circadian Rhythm and Sleep

The circadian rhythm is an internal timing system that coordinates alertness, hormone release, temperature, and sleep. Its central clock is the suprachiasmatic nucleus in the hypothalamus. Light reaching specialized retinal cells helps synchronize that clock with daylight.

Two processes influence sleep need: circadian timing and homeostatic sleep pressure, which rises while a person is awake. Evening light can delay the clock, whereas consistent morning light and a regular wake time support a stable daily pattern. Caffeine and long naps may temporarily weaken the usual pressure to sleep.
