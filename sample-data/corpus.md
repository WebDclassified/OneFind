# Citrus and vitamin C

Oranges, lemons, and grapefruits are citrus fruits prized for their high vitamin C content.
Vitamin C supports the immune system and helps the body absorb iron from plant foods.
A single medium orange provides most of an adult's recommended daily vitamin C intake.

# Running shoes and cushioning

Modern running shoes use foam midsoles to absorb impact and return energy to the runner.
Carbon-fiber plates embedded in racing shoes improve propulsion economy over long distances.
Rotating between two pairs of training shoes may reduce repetitive stress injuries.

# How photosynthesis works

Plants convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen inside chloroplasts.
Chlorophyll pigments absorb red and blue wavelengths of light while reflecting green light.
The light-dependent reactions split water molecules, releasing oxygen as a by-product.

# Sourdough fermentation basics

Sourdough bread rises through wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria rather than commercial yeast.
A starter culture must be fed flour and water daily to stay active and leaven dough.
Long cold fermentation develops the tangy flavor and open crumb characteristic of artisan loaves.
