# Building an Emergency Fund

An emergency fund is cash reserved for short-term, unavoidable expenses such as urgent repairs, temporary income loss, or a medical bill. A common starting target is several months of essential expenses, adjusted for income stability, insurance, family support, and access to credit.

Keep the fund in an accessible, low-risk account rather than tying it up in volatile investments. Automate small transfers after payday, record the target amount, and avoid treating the balance as everyday spending money. A separate sinking fund is better for predictable annual costs.
