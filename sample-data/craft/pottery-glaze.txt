Pottery Glazing and Vitrification

A ceramic glaze combines silica, fluxes, and stabilizers. During firing, the flux lowers the melting temperature so silica can partially vitrify into a glassy coating. Cone 6 is a common mid-range firing reference for many clay bodies, but the correct cone depends on the clay and intended surface.

Glaze chemistry affects melt, crazing, pinholing, and color. Test tiles should record clay body, application thickness, firing schedule, and cone. A successful recipe behaves consistently; copying only the listed ingredients without those process details is unreliable.
